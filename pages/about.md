## Project description

This is the GitHub repository for our CollectionBuilder Site. Our project is a collection of sunset and sunrise photographs. Focusing on photographic prints, this collection features a range of photographic techniques, including images produced from photochrom processes and lantern slides. The goal of this project is to capture the ways sunsets and sunrises have been depicted across time and mediums. Images are selected by looking at vibrancy, choosing pieces that catch the eye, and paying special attention to light contrasts in black and white photographs. While this collection focused primarily on photographs, the line between artistic renderings and traditional photographs are not all clear. Photographs produced in mediums such as photochrom prints toe this line toward a more illustrative look and are still represented in the collection.
